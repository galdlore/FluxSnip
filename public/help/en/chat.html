Google Chat

Templates on this tab are used only in Google Chat, on chat.google.com and in a Chat panel embedded in Gmail.

How to create, save, and search templates is on the overview.

Insert

Focus the message box, type the keyword, and press Tab. Or open the picker (default Alt+J) and press Enter. Chat has no To, Cc, or subject fields.

Formatting Chat can keep

The editor warns you when the body uses formatting Chat cannot keep. Open Preview and read Google Chat Formatted Preview before you save.
