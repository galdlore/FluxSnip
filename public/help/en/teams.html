Microsoft Teams

Templates on this tab are used only in Teams. They are not shared with Gmail or Google Chat.

How to create, save, and search templates is on the overview.

Where it works

  • Company Teams on teams.microsoft.com and teams.cloud.microsoft, including the installed PWA window
  • A 1:1 or group chat compose box
  • A channel’s new post and its reply box

Click the message box, type the keyword, and press Tab. Or press the picker shortcut (default Alt+J) and choose a template with Enter. The text is inserted at the caret.

Formatting

The body can include bold, lists, links, and tables. Paste a table from Excel or Word, then check Preview before you save. Teams shows tables in the message. Undo after insert follows Teams’ own editor.

There are no To, Cc, subject, or From fields on a Teams template. Use {{date}}, {{time}}, and {{weekday}} in the body. Recipient variables such as {{to.name}} are for Gmail.

If nothing is inserted

Confirm Teams is enabled on Settings, and that the keyword matches exactly, including case. Tab is ignored while an IME is converting. The search box at the top of Teams is not a message box, so keywords there are left alone.
